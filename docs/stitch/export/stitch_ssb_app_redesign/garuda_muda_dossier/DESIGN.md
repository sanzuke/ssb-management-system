## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the discipline, warmth, and documentary rigor of physical Indonesian grassroots football academies (*Sekolah Sepak Bola*). It eschews the gamified badges, glossy neon gradients, and sterile fintech dashboards typical of contemporary sports apps. Instead, it adopts the tactile, meticulous quality of an archivist's ledger or a physical progress notebook (*Buku Rapor Siswa*).

The aesthetic balances institutional authority with the vibrant spirit of youth athletics:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, pedagogical, warm, grounded, and legible under direct sunlight.
- **Visual Style:** Structured tactile minimalism with hairline field boundaries (*garis kapur*), clear tabular data structures, physical card cutouts, and crisp document-grade contrasts.
- **Ergonomic Context:** High-contrast daylight legibility, rapid one-handed inputs by coaches standing pitch-side, sweat- and glare-resistant touch targets, and unambiguous administrative legibility for parents and regional evaluators.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical pitch markings, boot-worn turf, printed report cards, and field stationery:

- **Primary (`#1B4D36` - Deep Turf Green):** Used for mastheads, core navigation, structural headers, high-level credentials, and prominent identity marks.
- **Accent / Primary Action (`#F0C23B` - Field Ochre Yellow):** High-visibility touch targets, submit actions, active state markers, and primary toggles. Always paired with dark ink (`#14201A`) text for AAA contrast.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F4F6EF` - Chalk White):** Warm, low-strain off-white reminiscent of unbleached archival paper; softens outdoor glare.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Discrete tactile containers for player files, attendance grids, and evaluation metrics.
- **Ink & Typography:**
  - High-Emphasis Ink: `#14201A` (Stylo Black-Green) for headlines, body, and metrics.
  - Medium-Emphasis Ink: `#4A5A52` (Muted Moss Slate) for subheaders, labels, and secondary metadata.
- **Structural Lines:**
  - Subtle Field Line / Border: `#DDE2D5`
  - Chalk Pitch Guideline: `#E3E8DD`
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Positive / Mastered: `#2A8A57` (Fresh Grass Green)
  - Comparison / Benchmark / In-Review: `#C0841A` (Pitch Amber)
  - Alert / Absence / Critical: `#B3372A` (Vermilion Red)

## Typography

The typography unites human character with clinical outdoor legibility:

- **Headings (Bricolage Grotesque):** Expressive, structured, and confident. Headings must always be set in sentence case to maintain a collegial, instructional tone rather than an aggressive marketing aesthetic.
- **Body & Data (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next):** Built specifically for hyper-legibility across vision ranges and harsh outdoor sunlight. Unambiguous letterforms (distinct 0 vs O, 1 vs l vs I) ensure coaches do not misread assessment ratings or player IDs.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) are strictly enforced across rating selectors, attendance percentages, player heights, and physical metrics to maintain vertical column alignment in tabular reports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a split-column technical dossier or sports ledger:
- **Mobile Handheld (Coach Pitch-side View):** Single-column stack with sticky top sub-navigation and fixed sticky bottom interaction anchors. Content gutters sit at `0.75rem` (12px) to maximize real estate for horizontal rating pills and data grids.
- **Tablet / Desktop (Evaluator & Parent Archive View):** Multi-pane split architecture (e.g., player list roster on the left 4 columns, detailed performance card on the right 8 columns).
- **Rhythm & Touch Standards:** All interactive inputs adhere strictly to a minimum touch bounding box of 48x48px, preventing misclicks when coaches operate devices while moving on the field.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cards, artificial neon glows, and heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated strictly through tactile physical stratification:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Matte Chalk Canvas (`#F4F6EF`).
  - Container / Sheet: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border in `#DDE2D5`.
  - Inset Well: `#ECEFE6` for recessed indicator bars, empty photo containers, and unselected segmented touch banks.
- **Hairline Boundaries (*Garis Kapur*):** 1px crisp borders delineate card bounds and cell partitions, referencing pitch lines chalked onto turf.
- **Tactile Pressed States:** Interactive elements drop 1px along the Y-axis with a darkened bottom-border adjustment rather than an expanding shadow blur, reinforcing the feel of a physical stamp or punch-card button.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, rounded corners (`0.5rem` / 8px for standard components, up to `1rem` / 16px for card containers) paired with purposeful pill silhouettes:
- **Cards & Data Modules:** 8px to 16px radius, cleanly cut, evoking printed certificates and laminated ledger files.
- **Interaction Targets (Pills):** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for single-character attendance tokens (H, I, S, A) and segmented score selectors (1–10). This contrasts against rectilinear cards and signals immediate finger affordance.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Field Action Buttons
- **Primary Field Button:** High-visibility `#F0C23B` background, `#14201A` text in `label-lg`, 48px minimum height, 1px solid `#D8A826` stroke. Active state uses a 1px downward shift.
- **Secondary Ledger Button:** Deep turf `#1B4D36` background, `#F4F6EF` text, 48px height.
- **Outline Action:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#DDE2D5` border, `#14201A` text.

### 2. Segmented Attendance Quad-Selector
- An inline 4-way segmented pill group:
  - **Hadir (H):** Active fill `#2A8A57` with white text.
  - **Izin (I):** Active fill `#C0841A` with white text.
  - **Sakit (S):** Active fill `#1B4D36` with white text.
  - **Alpa (A):** Active fill `#B3372A` with white text.
- Each quadrant retains a minimum 48x48px hit area, accompanied by bold single-letter codes and explicit subtitles below.

### 3. Metric Selector (1–10 Segmented Touch Bank)
- A horizontally scrolling or two-row wrapped numeric selector (1 to 10).
- Unselected buttons: `#FFFFFF` background, `#DDE2D5` border, `#4A5A52` text.
- Selected state: High-contrast `#1B4D36` fill with `#FFFFFF` tabular numerals, or `#F0C23B` fill with `#14201A` ink for peak ratings (9–10).

### 4. Tactile Player ID Card (*Kartu Pemain*)
- Physical dossier styling with `#FFFFFF` background and `#DDE2D5` border.
- Header row with student identifier (`SSB-0012`) and age group pill (`KU-10`).
- Left aspect: Square, crisp player portrait with hairline border.
- Right aspect: Name in `headline-sm`, primary position tag (e.g., "Gelandang Bertahan"), and verification QR code.
- Footer strip: Attendance percentage and latest technical index rendered in `metric-display`.

### 5. Sticky Bottom Action & Progress Bar
- Fixed persistent bar at the screen base.
- `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp top border (`#DDE2D5`).
- Left side: Completion status (e.g., "3 dari 17 indikator dinilai") alongside a segmented horizontal bar in `#1B4D36`.
- Right side: High-contrast 48px yellow action button ("Simpan Nilai").

### 6. Technical Radar & Progression Graphics
- Polygonal evaluation charts drawn using high-contrast 1.5px lines in `#1B4D36` with a translucent turf-tinted fill (`rgba(27, 77, 54, 0.15)`).
- Pitch benchmark comparison overlays plotted with dashed 1px lines in `#C0841A`.
- Avoids all glossy radial gradients or pseudo-3D perspective planes.
